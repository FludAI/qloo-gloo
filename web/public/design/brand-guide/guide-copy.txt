05 · Use
Rules of thumb
Do
Use the on-dark logo over photos and the camera view, under a dark wash.
Use gold only on the toes, the token paw and the five-tick meter when it is full.
Keep the five toes as the five stickiness ticks everywhere they appear.
Use the mono ink logo when only one color is available.
Don’t
Don’t put the teal paw on mid-tone or busy backgrounds.
Don’t make the palm gold in the full logo. Only the all-gold token does that.
Don’t add outlines, glows, shadows or gradients to the wordmark.
Don’t stretch, rotate, or rearrange the paw and wordmark.
